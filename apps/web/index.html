Antenna

Track the signals that matter.

A personal signal-monitoring app. Collects live market, operations, and research signals into an installable visual workspace with clear source attribution and freshness, while MCP agents manage and use the same owner-scoped data.

How Antenna uses Google user data

Antenna uses Google Sign-In only to authenticate your account. It receives your name, email address, and profile picture to identify your account and display your profile. It does not request access to Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive, or other Google account data.
