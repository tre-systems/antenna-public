Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Antenna is self-hosted software. The person or organisation that made this deployment available is its operator and data controller. This notice explains the data an Antenna deployment processes; the operator must supplement it with their identity, contact details, legal bases, processors, retention periods, and jurisdiction-specific rights.

Who this applies to

Interactive access is limited to Google accounts on an operator-maintained allowlist. If you are not on the allowlist, you cannot create an account or use authenticated features.

Information we collect

Account and sign-in

When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information from Google, including your name, email address, and profile image. We use this to identify your account, enforce the access allowlist, and display your profile in the app.

Collections, signals, and agent access

Antenna stores the collections, signals, configuration, fetched data points, status, and setup requests associated with your account. If you use MCP or other agent integrations, we store OAuth grants and access metadata needed to authenticate those clients on your behalf.

Prompts and product learning

Text you submit when adding signals or planning collections may be stored so Antenna can improve matching and setup flows. Treat prompts as product data, not as a confidential messaging channel.

Technical and security data

We collect standard service logs and error telemetry (for example through Sentry) to keep the service reliable. We also record coarse app-usage events to understand how Antenna is used. Telemetry is filtered to avoid cookies, authorization headers, request bodies, and other sensitive content where possible.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session
  • Fetch, store, and display the signals in your collections
  • Enforce visibility, source policy, and sharing rules
  • Operate MCP and agent integrations you authorize
  • Monitor reliability, abuse, and security incidents
  • Improve the service

We do not sell your personal information.

How we store and protect data

Antenna runs on Cloudflare Workers with D1, R2, and related Cloudflare services. Google provider access, refresh, and ID tokens are discarded before account persistence. MCP OAuth grants remain high-trust service data. Sessions, collections, and signals are private by default. Public or shared views expose only what the Worker source policy allows.

Retention

We retain account and collection data while your account remains active and as needed to operate the service. The operator is responsible for publishing concrete retention periods and deletion procedures. You can sign out at any time; contact the operator if you need account deletion.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run Antenna (for example Cloudflare and Google), when you explicitly enable a shared or public collection link, or when required by law. Shared links are bearer capabilities and should be treated like secrets.

Your choices

  • Sign out of Antenna at any time from the app
  • Revoke Google or MCP access from Antenna settings or your Google account
  • Keep collections private unless you deliberately share them
  • Contact the operator to request access removal or account deletion

Children

Antenna is not directed at children and is not offered to users under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Use the contact method supplied by the operator of this deployment. The open-source maintainers do not control or have access to independently hosted Antenna instances.
