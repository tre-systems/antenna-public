# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Antenna to be safe, respectful, and constructive.
Contributors must:

- treat other people with empathy and professional respect
- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks
- welcome questions and differing experience levels
- respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or confidential information
- accept proportionate moderation when conduct harms the community

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository spaces and when representing the project
elsewhere. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the
repository's private security-reporting channel. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Decisions will
consider severity, repetition, impact, and the safety of affected people.
